<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">1. Name of Procedure</h2>
<p><strong>[Write the complete name of the procedure here]</strong></p>
<p>Example: Hand Washing Procedure / Temperature Recording Procedure / Blood Pressure Measurement Procedure / ORS Preparation Procedure.</p>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">2. Definition</h2>
<p>Definition means a clear and simple explanation of the procedure. It should explain what the procedure is and why it is performed.</p>

<div style="background:#e8f0fe;padding:15px;border-radius:8px;margin:15px 0;">
<strong>Example Format:</strong>
<p style="margin-bottom:0;">[Procedure name] is a nursing procedure performed to [main purpose] by using [method/equipment] in order to promote health, prevent complications, and provide safe nursing care.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">3. Purpose / Objectives</h2>
<ul>
<li>To provide safe and effective nursing care.</li>
<li>To assess the health condition of the patient/client.</li>
<li>To prevent infection and complications.</li>
<li>To promote comfort and hygiene.</li>
<li>To assist in diagnosis, treatment, or health promotion.</li>
<li>To educate the patient/client and family members.</li>
<li>To maintain accurate records of nursing care.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">4. Indications</h2>
<p>Indications are the conditions or situations where the procedure is required.</p>
<ul>
<li>When assessment of patient/client is needed.</li>
<li>When prevention of infection is required.</li>
<li>When health education or demonstration is needed.</li>
<li>When ordered by doctor or instructed by clinical supervisor.</li>
<li>When patient/client requires nursing care or monitoring.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">5. Contraindications</h2>
<p>Contraindications are conditions where the procedure should not be done or should be done carefully.</p>
<ul>
<li>Do not perform the procedure if patient refuses, unless emergency care is required.</li>
<li>Do not perform without proper articles or aseptic precautions.</li>
<li>Do not perform if it may harm the patient/client.</li>
<li>Follow institutional guidelines and instructor’s direction.</li>
<li>For special conditions, consult senior nurse/doctor/clinical instructor.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">7. Preparation of Patient / Client</h2>
<ul>
<li>Identify the patient/client correctly.</li>
<li>Introduce yourself to the patient/client.</li>
<li>Explain the procedure in simple language.</li>
<li>Take consent before performing the procedure.</li>
<li>Provide privacy and maintain dignity.</li>
<li>Position the patient/client comfortably.</li>
<li>Assess the general condition of the patient/client.</li>
<li>Remove fear and anxiety by giving reassurance.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">8. Preparation of Nurse</h2>
<ul>
<li>Review the procedure and purpose.</li>
<li>Check doctor’s order if required.</li>
<li>Collect all necessary articles.</li>
<li>Wash hands before procedure.</li>
<li>Wear gloves or protective equipment if needed.</li>
<li>Maintain aseptic technique wherever required.</li>
<li>Ensure safety, comfort, and privacy of patient/client.</li>
<li>Follow institutional policy and instructor’s guidance.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">10. Precautions</h2>
<ul>
<li>Always perform hand hygiene before and after procedure.</li>
<li>Use clean and correct equipment.</li>
<li>Maintain aseptic technique where required.</li>
<li>Do not leave the patient/client unattended if condition is unstable.</li>
<li>Explain each step to reduce fear and anxiety.</li>
<li>Observe for discomfort, pain, dizziness, bleeding, allergy, or abnormal response.</li>
<li>Do not reuse contaminated articles.</li>
<li>Dispose of waste properly.</li>
<li>Document immediately after procedure.</li>
<li>Report abnormal findings to senior nurse/doctor/clinical instructor.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">11. After Care</h2>
<ul>
<li>Make the patient/client comfortable.</li>
<li>Replace articles to proper place after cleaning.</li>
<li>Dispose of waste safely.</li>
<li>Remove gloves if used and perform hand hygiene.</li>
<li>Observe the patient/client for any discomfort or complication.</li>
<li>Provide health education if required.</li>
<li>Inform the patient/client about next step or follow-up.</li>
<li>Report any abnormal findings immediately.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">12. Documentation / Recording</h2>
<p>Documentation should be done immediately after completing the procedure.</p>
<ul>
<li>Date and time of procedure.</li>
<li>Name of procedure performed.</li>
<li>Patient/client identification details.</li>
<li>Important findings or observations.</li>
<li>Patient/client response to procedure.</li>
<li>Any abnormal finding or complication.</li>
<li>Health education given.</li>
<li>Name and signature of student nurse.</li>
<li>Signature of clinical instructor if required.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">13. Health Education</h2>
<ul>
<li>Explain the purpose of the procedure to the patient/client and family.</li>
<li>Teach preventive measures related to the procedure.</li>
<li>Explain warning signs that require medical help.</li>
<li>Encourage follow-up visit if needed.</li>
<li>Teach hygiene, safety, nutrition, rest, medication compliance, or self-care as applicable.</li>
<li>Advise not to follow unsafe home remedies or self-medication where applicable.</li>
</ul>
