<h2>2. Introduction</h2>
<p>
A family folder is a complete record of a selected family prepared during community health nursing practical. This family folder is based on a family having a postnatal mother. The postnatal period begins after delivery and continues up to 6 weeks. During this period, the mother and newborn require proper care, nutrition, hygiene, breastfeeding support, immunization, rest and observation for danger signs.
</p>

<h2>3. Objectives</h2>
<ul>
<li>To assess the health status of the selected family.</li>
<li>To assess the postnatal mother and newborn baby.</li>
<li>To identify postnatal health problems and risk factors.</li>
<li>To assess breastfeeding practices and newborn care.</li>
<li>To provide health education regarding postnatal care, nutrition, hygiene and family planning.</li>
<li>To prepare nursing care plan according to identified problems.</li>
<li>To promote maternal and newborn health.</li>
</ul>

<h2>16. Health Education Given</h2>
<ul>
<li>Take adequate rest and sleep during postnatal period.</li>
<li>Eat balanced diet including dal, milk, fruits, vegetables and iron-rich foods.</li>
<li>Continue IFA and calcium tablets as advised.</li>
<li>Maintain perineal hygiene and change pads regularly.</li>
<li>Wash hands before breastfeeding and handling newborn.</li>
<li>Practice exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months.</li>
<li>Do not give water, honey, ghutti or outside milk to baby.</li>
<li>Keep baby warm and maintain clean cord care.</li>
<li>Complete newborn immunization as per schedule.</li>
<li>Attend postnatal check-up at health facility.</li>
<li>Use suitable family planning method after counselling.</li>
</ul>

<h2>17. Danger Signs Explained</h2>
<h3>Danger Signs in Postnatal Mother</h3>
<ul>
<li>Heavy vaginal bleeding</li>
<li>High fever</li>
<li>Foul-smelling vaginal discharge</li>
<li>Severe abdominal pain</li>
<li>Severe headache or blurred vision</li>
<li>Breast pain, redness or swelling</li>
<li>Difficulty in breathing</li>
</ul>

<h3>Danger Signs in Newborn</h3>
<ul>
<li>Poor feeding or unable to suck</li>
<li>Fast breathing or difficulty in breathing</li>
<li>Fever or low body temperature</li>
<li>Yellow discoloration of skin or eyes</li>
<li>Convulsions</li>
<li>Umbilical redness, pus or foul smell</li>
<li>Lethargy or excessive crying</li>
</ul>
