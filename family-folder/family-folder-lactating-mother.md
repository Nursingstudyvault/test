<h2>2. Introduction</h2>
<p>
A family folder is a systematic record of a selected family prepared during community health nursing practical. This family folder is based on a family having a lactating mother. A lactating mother requires adequate nutrition, rest, personal hygiene, breast care, emotional support and correct breastfeeding practices to maintain her health and promote proper growth and development of the infant.
</p>

<h2>3. Objectives</h2>
<ul>
<li>To assess the health status of the selected family.</li>
<li>To assess the lactating mother and infant.</li>
<li>To identify health problems related to lactation, nutrition and hygiene.</li>
<li>To assess breastfeeding technique and feeding practices.</li>
<li>To provide health education regarding exclusive breastfeeding and maternal nutrition.</li>
<li>To prepare nursing care plan according to identified problems.</li>
<li>To promote maternal and child health.</li>
</ul>

<h2>16. Health Education Given</h2>
<ul>
<li>Take balanced diet with extra calories and protein during lactation.</li>
<li>Include milk, dal, green leafy vegetables, fruits, pulses, eggs or other protein-rich foods according to availability.</li>
<li>Drink adequate water and fluids.</li>
<li>Take proper rest and sleep.</li>
<li>Continue exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months.</li>
<li>Do not give water, honey, ghutti, tea or outside milk before 6 months.</li>
<li>Wash hands before breastfeeding and handling infant.</li>
<li>Maintain breast hygiene and observe for nipple cracks or breast pain.</li>
<li>Burp the baby after feeding.</li>
<li>Complete immunization according to schedule.</li>
<li>Use suitable family planning method after counselling.</li>
</ul>

<h2>17. Danger Signs Explained</h2>

<h3>Danger Signs in Lactating Mother</h3>
<ul>
<li>High fever</li>
<li>Breast pain, redness or swelling</li>
<li>Cracked nipple with severe pain</li>
<li>Pus discharge from breast</li>
<li>Severe weakness or dizziness</li>
<li>Heavy vaginal bleeding</li>
<li>Severe abdominal pain</li>
</ul>

<h3>Danger Signs in Infant</h3>
<ul>
<li>Poor feeding or unable to suck</li>
<li>Fast breathing or difficulty in breathing</li>
<li>Fever or low body temperature</li>
<li>Repeated vomiting</li>
<li>Convulsions</li>
<li>Yellow discoloration of skin or eyes</li>
<li>Lethargy or excessive crying</li>
</ul>
