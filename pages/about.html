About Nursing Study Vault

Welcome to Nursing Study Vault! — your trusted companion in nursing education. We are dedicated to helping ANM, GNM, and BSc Nursing students excel in their academic practicals by providing high-quality, ready-made nursing files.

Our Mission: To make nursing practical file preparation easy, accessible, and stress-free for every nursing student — completely free of cost.

What We Offer

  • Nursing Care Plans — Detailed plans with assessment, diagnosis, planning, implementation, and evaluation
  • Surgical Care Plans — Pre-operative and post-operative nursing care plans
  • Case Studies — In-depth patient case analysis with nursing management
  • Health Talks — Ready-made scripts for community health presentations
  • Health Education — Patient and community teaching materials
  • Procedures — Step-by-step nursing procedures with rationale
  • Assignments — Properly formatted assignment files
  • Nursing Notes — Comprehensive study notes and summaries
  • Family Folders — Community health nursing family survey formats

Who Is This For?

  • ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery) students
  • GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) students
  • BSc Nursing students
  • Nursing educators looking for teaching resources
