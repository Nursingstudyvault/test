<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">📖 Introduction</h2>
<p>Adolescence is an important period of life between childhood and adulthood. During this stage, many physical, emotional, mental, and social changes occur. Proper care during adolescence helps in healthy growth, prevention of anemia, maintenance of personal hygiene, good mental health, prevention of harmful habits, and development of responsible behaviour.</p>

<h2 style="color:#1557b0;border-left:5px solid #1a73e8;padding-left:12px;margin:30px 0 15px;">📖 Content Matter</h2>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">1. Meaning of Adolescence</h3>
<p>Adolescence is the period of life between childhood and adulthood. According to WHO, adolescence is the age group between <strong>10 to 19 years</strong>. It is a period of rapid growth, development, maturity, emotional changes, and personality development.</p>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">2. Physical Changes During Adolescence</h3>
<ul>
<li>Rapid increase in height and weight.</li>
<li>Development of secondary sexual characteristics.</li>
<li>Growth of hair in underarms and pubic area.</li>
<li>Voice changes in boys.</li>
<li>Development of breasts and menstruation in girls.</li>
<li>Increase in sweating and body odour.</li>
<li>Acne or pimples may appear due to hormonal changes.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">3. Emotional and Psychological Changes</h3>
<ul>
<li>Mood swings and emotional sensitivity.</li>
<li>Desire for independence.</li>
<li>Curiosity about body changes and relationships.</li>
<li>Influence of friends and peer pressure.</li>
<li>Feeling shy, confused, angry, or anxious sometimes.</li>
<li>Need for support from parents, teachers, and health workers.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">4. Personal Hygiene During Adolescence</h3>
<ul>
<li>Take bath daily with clean water and soap.</li>
<li>Wear clean and washed clothes daily.</li>
<li>Brush teeth twice daily.</li>
<li>Keep nails short and clean.</li>
<li>Wash hands before eating and after using toilet.</li>
<li>Use separate towel, comb, and personal items.</li>
<li>Wash face regularly to prevent acne and skin infection.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">5. Menstrual Hygiene for Adolescent Girls</h3>
<ul>
<li>Menstruation is a normal biological process.</li>
<li>Use clean sanitary pad or clean absorbent material during menstruation.</li>
<li>Change pad every 4-6 hours or whenever soaked.</li>
<li>Wash private parts with clean water.</li>
<li>Dispose used pads safely by wrapping properly.</li>
<li>Do not feel shame or fear; ask mother, teacher, nurse, or health worker for guidance.</li>
<li>Consult health worker if bleeding is very heavy, periods are very painful, or there is foul-smelling discharge.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">6. Nutrition During Adolescence</h3>
<p>Adolescents need more nutrients because this is a period of rapid growth. A balanced diet helps in proper body growth, strong bones, good immunity, prevention of anemia, and better concentration in studies.</p>

<ul>
<li>Eat cereals, pulses, vegetables, fruits, milk, curd, egg, meat, fish, or paneer according to availability.</li>
<li>Include iron-rich foods like green leafy vegetables, jaggery, dates, pulses, groundnuts, eggs, and meat.</li>
<li>Take vitamin C-rich foods like lemon, amla, guava, orange, and tomato to improve iron absorption.</li>
<li>Drink adequate clean water.</li>
<li>Avoid junk food, excess fried food, sugary drinks, and packaged snacks.</li>
<li>Do not skip breakfast.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">7. Prevention of Anemia</h3>
<ul>
<li>Eat iron-rich foods daily.</li>
<li>Take Iron Folic Acid tablets as advised under school health programme.</li>
<li>Take deworming tablet as advised by health worker.</li>
<li>Maintain personal hygiene and use footwear to prevent worm infestation.</li>
<li>Girls should take extra care during menstruation and eat nutritious food.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">8. Mental Health and Stress Management</h3>
<ul>
<li>Share problems with parents, teachers, friends, counsellor, or health worker.</li>
<li>Do regular exercise, yoga, sports, or outdoor activity.</li>
<li>Sleep 7-9 hours daily.</li>
<li>Avoid excessive mobile phone and social media use.</li>
<li>Develop hobbies like reading, music, drawing, gardening, or sports.</li>
<li>Do not compare yourself with others.</li>
<li>Seek help if feeling sad, hopeless, fearful, or stressed for many days.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">9. Healthy Lifestyle During Adolescence</h3>
<ul>
<li>Follow daily routine and time management.</li>
<li>Maintain regular study and rest schedule.</li>
<li>Participate in sports and physical activities.</li>
<li>Respect parents, teachers, elders, and friends.</li>
<li>Maintain good personal habits and discipline.</li>
<li>Use internet and mobile phone safely and responsibly.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">10. Prevention of Harmful Habits</h3>
<ul>
<li>Avoid tobacco, gutkha, alcohol, smoking, and drugs.</li>
<li>Say no to peer pressure and bad company.</li>
<li>Do not take any medicine or substance without advice.</li>
<li>Avoid unsafe online contacts and sharing personal information.</li>
<li>Seek help from trusted adults if someone forces or threatens you.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">11. Role of Parents, Teachers and Health Workers</h3>
<ul>
<li>Provide correct information about body changes.</li>
<li>Support adolescents emotionally.</li>
<li>Encourage healthy food and hygiene habits.</li>
<li>Guide adolescents regarding safe behaviour.</li>
<li>Identify stress, depression, abuse, anemia, malnutrition, or harmful habits early.</li>
<li>Refer adolescents to health facility when needed.</li>
</ul>
