## 📚 CONTENT

### 1. Introduction (3-4 minutes)

Good morning children! Today we will learn about **Personal Hygiene**. Hygiene means keeping our body clean. When we stay clean, we stay healthy and strong.

### 2. Definition of Personal Hygiene

**Personal hygiene** means taking care of your body and keeping it clean. It includes washing hands, brushing teeth, bathing, wearing clean clothes, and keeping nails short.

### 3. Components of Personal Hygiene

| S.No | Component | Key Point |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Hand Hygiene | Wash before eating & after toilet |
| 2 | Oral Hygiene | Brush teeth twice daily |
| 3 | Bathing | Daily bath with soap & water |
| 4 | Hair Care | Wash 2-3 times per week |
| 5 | Nail Care | Trim weekly, keep short |
| 6 | Foot Care | Wash daily, clean socks |
| 7 | Clean Clothing | Wear washed & ironed clothes |

### 4. Hand Washing — Most Important Habit

**When to wash hands:**
- Before eating food
- After using the toilet
- After playing outside
- After coughing or sneezing
- When hands look dirty

**10 Steps of Hand Washing (WHO):**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Wet hands with water |
| 2 | Apply soap |
| 3 | Rub palm to palm |
| 4 | Right palm over left back & vice versa |
| 5 | Palm to palm, fingers interlaced |
| 6 | Back of fingers to opposing palms |
| 7 | Rotational rubbing of thumbs |
| 8 | Rotational rubbing of fingertips |
| 9 | Rinse with water |
| 10 | Dry with clean towel |

**Duration:** At least 20-30 seconds

### 5. Oral Hygiene

- Brush teeth **twice daily** (morning & night)
- Use soft toothbrush & fluoride toothpaste
- Brush for 2 minutes
- Clean tongue daily
- Rinse mouth after eating
- Visit dentist every 6 months

### 6. Bathing & Skin Care

- Take bath **daily** with clean water and soap
- Pay attention to underarms, groin, and feet
- Dry body with clean personal towel
- Wear clean undergarments daily

### 7. Nail Care

- Trim nails **once every week**
- Keep nails short and clean
- Use nail brush to clean under nails
- **Do not bite nails** — germs enter stomach

### 8. Hair Care

- Wash hair 2-3 times per week
- Comb hair daily
- Use personal comb only — do not share
- Check for lice weekly

### 9. Clean Clothing

- Wear **clean, washed, ironed** uniform daily
- Change undergarments daily
- Wear clean socks every day

### 10. Diseases Due to Poor Hygiene

| Disease | Cause |
|---------|-------|
| Diarrhea | Dirty hands, contaminated food |
| Typhoid | Contaminated food & water |
| Dental caries | Not brushing teeth |
| Skin infections | Not bathing regularly |
| Worm infestation | Dirty nails, barefoot walking |
| Lice infestation | Sharing combs, unwashed hair |

## ✅ SUMMARY — 5 Golden Rules

1. ✋ **Wash hands** with soap and water frequently
2. 🦷 **Brush teeth** twice daily
3. 🚿 **Take bath** every day
4. ✂️ **Keep nails** short and clean
5. 👕 **Wear clean clothes** daily

> **Stay Clean, Stay Healthy, Stay Happy!**
