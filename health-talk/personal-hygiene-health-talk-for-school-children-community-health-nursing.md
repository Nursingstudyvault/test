<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">1. Introduction (3-4 Minutes)</h3>
<p><strong>Greeting:</strong> "Good morning, respected teachers and my dear children! My name is [Your Name] and I am a nursing student from [College Name]. Today, I am going to talk to you about something very important — something that keeps you healthy, strong, and happy every single day. Can anyone guess what I am talking about?"</p>
<p><strong>Ice-Breaking:</strong> "How many of you washed your hands before coming to school today? Raise your hands! How many brushed your teeth this morning? Excellent! Today we will learn why these small habits are so important."</p>

</blockquote>

<!-- ==================== DEFINITION ==================== -->
<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">2. Definition of Personal Hygiene (2-3 Minutes)</h3>
<p>Personal hygiene is defined as the <strong>science of health and its preservation</strong>. It involves all measures taken by an individual to maintain bodily cleanliness, protect against infections, and promote physical, mental, and social well-being. (Reference: K. Park, Textbook of Preventive and Social Medicine)</p>

<h4>The 5 Critical Moments for Hand Washing:</h4>
<ol>
<li><strong>Before eating food</strong> — Prevent germs entering mouth</li>
<li><strong>After using toilet</strong> — Wash away germs from stool/urine</li>
<li><strong>After playing outside</strong> — Remove dirt from playground</li>
<li><strong>After coughing/sneezing</strong> — Prevent spreading germs</li>
<li><strong>When hands look dirty</strong> — Visible dirt = definite germs</li>
</ol>

<!-- ==================== BATHING ==================== -->
<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">6. Bathing and Skin Care</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Frequency:</strong> Daily bath with clean water & mild soap</li>
<li><strong>Special attention:</strong> Underarms, groin, neck, behind ears, between toes</li>
<li><strong>After bath:</strong> Dry thoroughly with personal clean towel</li>
<li><strong>Benefits:</strong> Removes dirt & sweat, prevents body odor & skin infections, gives freshness</li>
</ul>

<!-- ==================== NAIL CARE ==================== -->
<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">7. Nail Care</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Trim nails</strong> once every week</li>
<li><strong>Clean under nails</strong> daily with nail brush</li>
<li><strong>Do not bite nails</strong> — leads to worm infestation and infections</li>
<li><strong>Diseases:</strong> Roundworm, pinworm, hookworm, diarrhea</li>
</ul>
