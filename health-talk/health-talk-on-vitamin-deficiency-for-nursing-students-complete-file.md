<!-- ==================== DEFINITION ==================== -->
<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">2. Definition of Vitamin Deficiency (2-3 Minutes)</h3>
<p><strong>Vitamins</strong> are essential organic nutrients required in small amounts for normal growth, development, metabolism, immunity, and maintenance of body functions.</p>
<p><strong>Vitamin deficiency</strong> is a condition that occurs when the body does not get enough amount of one or more vitamins from diet, sunlight, or absorption, leading to specific signs, symptoms, and deficiency diseases.</p>

<h4>Common Causes of Vitamin Deficiency:</h4>
<ol>
<li><strong>Poor dietary intake</strong> — Not eating fruits, vegetables, milk, pulses, and protein-rich food</li>
<li><strong>Unbalanced diet</strong> — Eating mostly junk food or only one type of food</li>
<li><strong>Poverty and food insecurity</strong> — Inability to afford nutritious food</li>
<li><strong>Poor absorption</strong> — Intestinal diseases, diarrhea, worm infestation, malabsorption</li>
<li><strong>Increased body requirement</strong> — Pregnancy, lactation, childhood, adolescence, illness</li>
<li><strong>Lack of sunlight</strong> — Causes vitamin D deficiency</li>
<li><strong>Improper cooking</strong> — Overcooking may destroy water-soluble vitamins like vitamin C and B-complex</li>
</ol>

<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">6. Signs and Symptoms of Vitamin Deficiency</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>General weakness and tiredness:</strong> Person may feel low energy and unable to work properly</li>
<li><strong>Pale skin and dizziness:</strong> May occur in vitamin B12 or folate deficiency anemia</li>
<li><strong>Bleeding gums:</strong> Commonly seen in vitamin C deficiency</li>
<li><strong>Night blindness:</strong> Difficulty seeing in dim light due to vitamin A deficiency</li>
<li><strong>Bone pain and delayed growth:</strong> Seen in vitamin D deficiency</li>
<li><strong>Frequent infections:</strong> Poor immunity due to deficiency of vitamins A, C, D, and others</li>
<li><strong>Dry skin and hair fall:</strong> May occur due to poor nutrition and vitamin deficiency</li>
</ul>

<!-- ==================== NAIL CARE ==================== -->
<h3 style="color:#1a73e8;">7. Prevention of Vitamin Deficiency</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Eat a balanced diet</strong> including cereals, pulses, milk, fruits, vegetables, nuts, and protein-rich foods</li>
<li><strong>Include green leafy vegetables</strong> such as spinach, fenugreek, mustard leaves, and cabbage</li>
<li><strong>Eat seasonal fruits</strong> like guava, amla, orange, papaya, mango, and banana</li>
<li><strong>Take sunlight exposure</strong> for vitamin D, preferably in morning sunlight</li>
<li><strong>Avoid junk food</strong> because it fills the stomach but does not provide enough vitamins</li>
<li><strong>Do not overcook vegetables</strong> as excess heat can destroy some vitamins</li>
<li><strong>Maintain personal hygiene</strong> to prevent diarrhea and worm infestation, which may reduce nutrient absorption</li>
<li><strong>Take supplements only as advised</strong> by doctor, nurse, or health worker</li>
</ul>
